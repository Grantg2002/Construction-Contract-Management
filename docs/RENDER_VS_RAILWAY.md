# Render vs Railway Comparison

## Why Render is a Great Choice

### Render Advantages:
- ✅ **750 hours/month free** (vs Railway's 500)
- ✅ **Easy setup** - Great web UI
- ✅ **Good documentation** - Clear guides
- ✅ **Reliable** - Used by many companies
- ✅ **Free tier** - Perfect for testing

### Render Free Tier:
- 750 hours/month (enough for testing)
- 512MB RAM (enough for PDF processing)
- Spins down after 15 min inactivity (first request takes ~30 sec)
- Free subdomain (your-app.onrender.com)

### Railway Free Tier:
- 500 hours/month
- $5 credit/month
- Similar features

## Both Are Great Choices!

**Render** = More free hours, slightly slower cold starts
**Railway** = Slightly faster, less free hours

For your use case (PDF processing), **Render is perfect!** ✅

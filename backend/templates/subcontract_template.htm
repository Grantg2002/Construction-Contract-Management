8.        Insurance.  All subcontractors are to carry Workers Compensation Insurance, including Employer’s Liability to cover employees injuries or disease compensable under the Workers Compensation Statutes of the State of Michigan. In addition, all subcontractors are to carry a Comprehensive General Liability Policy to cover bodily injury to persons other than employees and for damage to tangible property. The subcontractor shall maintain minimum limits of liability in the amount of $1,000,000 per occurrence with a $2,000,000 general aggregate. Certificates of insurance to this effect are to be submitted to the office of Gulker Group LLC with acceptance of the contract. Required coverage as detailed in the Subcontractor Insurance requirements is mandatory and shall be maintained by the Subcontractor for the duration of the contract.
